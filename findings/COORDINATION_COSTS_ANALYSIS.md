## Reading

Coordination cost is real and stepwise: once a change leaves the “few files” band, review + comment volume jumps more than linearly. Decision *time* plateaus from medium to high — the extra cost shows up as more people and more messages, not proportionally longer clocks.

This JSON is GitHub-only volume. Informal-channel mentions (IRC / ML / Delving / Bitcointalk) are counted in cross-platform review weighting, not in these averages.
