## What is the Gini Coefficient?

The **Gini coefficient** measures inequality from 0 to 1:

- **0.0** = Perfect equality
- **1.0** = Perfect inequality

Common reading: **< 0.3** low, **0.3–0.6** moderate, **≥ 0.6** high/extreme.
