## 1. Agenda-setting vs decision (by era)

Informal channels put topics on the table. GitHub finalizes. Those are different jobs, and they swapped venues.

| Era | GitHub PRs | IRC mentions | Email | Delving | Bitcointalk | Discussed *before* PR | Flow |
|-----|-----------:|-------------:|------:|--------:|------------:|----------------------:|-----:|
| Early (2010–2014) | 3,766 | 0 | 179 | 0 | 180 | 5 | 0.001 |
| SegWit (2015–2017) | 4,594 | 4,165 | 2,116 | 0 | 149 | 2,615 | 0.569 |
| Taproot (2018–2021) | 8,141 | 8,504 | 3,790 | 0 | 169 | 4,477 | 0.550 |
| 2022+ | 8,821 | 7,710 | 727 | 535 | 161 | 21 | 0.002 |


All-time flow is **0.281** (7,126 PRs discussed off-GitHub first). That average is almost entirely **2015–2021 IRC/email**. 2022+ Delving and IRC are loud and almost never first.

**Cite as**: which era, which channel, before or after the PR number existed.  
**Do not cite**: “the community decided on Delving” as if that were SegWit-style agenda-setting.

## 4. Exit as selection, not collapse

**89.3%** of 7,971 contributors have no activity in 365 days (850 still active). That headline is true and misleading until split:

| Segment | n | 1-year exit |
|---------|--:|------------:|
| All contributors | 7,971 | 89.3% |
| Participants only (never authored a PR) | 5,356 | 93.3% |
| One-time (exactly 1 activity) | — | 91.4% (42.4% of all) |
| PR authors | 2,615 | 81.1% |
| High-quality authors (50%+ merge) | 879 | 82.3% |
| Established authors (5+ authored PRs a key holder merged) | 207 | 56.0% |
| … of whom maintainers | — | 19.0% |
| … of whom non-maintainers | — | 60.2% |

The process keeps a small continuing set. High merge-rate authors still leave at 82.3% — quality does not retain. Maintainers among the established set leave much less.

**Cite as**: selection into a thin continuing core.  
**Do not cite**: 91% exit as “the project is dying.”
