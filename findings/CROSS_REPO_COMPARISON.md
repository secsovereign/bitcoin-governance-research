## Implications

1. **BIPs is a wider door**: hundreds of unique mergers vs ~23 on Core.
2. **Power does not transfer cleanly**: high BIP-repo activity is a weak predictor of Core merge authority (20.0% top-10 overlap).
3. **Merge rates are similar** (~67.1% vs ~64.3%) even though concentration is not — BIPs distributes *who can merge*, Core concentrates it.
